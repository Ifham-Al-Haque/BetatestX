# UHub / UDrive Conventions

## Users vs Employees (do not confuse)

| Table       | Meaning                | Use for |
|------------|------------------------|--------|
| **`users`**   | **UHub account holders** | People who log into the UHub app. Auth, roles, “who is using the system,” IT staff (as app users), notifications for app users. |
| **`employees`** | **UDrive employees**      | HR/company employee records. Employee profiles, org chart, department, Asset Assignment, SIM “current user” (employee), company staff data. |

- **users** = UHub app login / account.
- **employees** = UDrive employee (company record).

When in doubt: “Can this person log into UHub?” → `users`. “Is this the company’s employee record?” → `employees`.
