💡 Testing Tips

  • Mobile Sidebar: Click the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-left to open the drawer
  • Navigation: Click any menu item - the sidebar should auto-close on mobile
  • Touch Targets: All buttons should be at least 44x44px for easy tapping
  • Forms: Input fields should be 16px font-size to prevent iOS zoom
  • Responsive: Resize your browser window to see breakpoints in action
  • DevTools: Press F12 in the iframe to inspect mobile-specific styles
